## `#` What is this place?

This site is my [[What is a Digital Garden|Digital Garden]]:

> [!QUOTE] ...a living, interconnected web of ideas, notes and reflections.

This is by no means a traditional blog with chronological posts, but a constantly evolving space where concepts grow, change and link to each other as they mature.

Here you'll find:

- notes and insights I write to myself, but freely visible.
- concepts, experiments, and seeds of ideas.
- interconnected thinking rather than linear storytelling.

This garden is meant to help me:

> [!QUOTE] ...think better, remember more, and build connections between topics.

 **think better, remember more, and build connections between topics** I like such as Data Engineering, AI, Learning Systems, Foreign Languages, Photography, Mathematics, Physics, Astronomy, and a ton more.

## `#` How to navigate

There's no strict "menu order" to follow in this site. It is built to be explored.

### `##` 🔍 Search

Use the 🔍 **search box** to jump to whatever term or idea you're curious about. I have some notes related to it, they will appear.

### `##` 🖇️ Interlinked notes

Many pages contain 🔙 **backlinks** to other concepts. This will allow you (me) to discover related ideas.

## `#` How this garden grows

This garden reflects how my thinking evolves, and that's the point:
- you'll see ideas in progress, not polished stuff.
- notes will be refined over time rather than archived.
- connections between notes matter more than topics or categories.

## `#` Why this format?

Unlike a blog timeline where the newest post always surfaces first, a [[What is a Digital Garden|Digital Garden]] is about paths, ideas, and growth patterns. That means:

- content is 🌱 evergreen.
- ideas evolve over time.
- nothing needs to be perfect before publishing it in the website.
