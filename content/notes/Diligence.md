`Diligence` is one of the fundamental principles on which the method of [[Young, S. (2020). Ultralearning: Master Hard Skills, Outsmart the Competition, and Accelerate Your Career]] by [[Scott H. Young]] is based. It consists of doing what you want to learn through practice, as opposed to [[Passive Learning]].
