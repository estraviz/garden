description:
date: 2025-10-04 23:23

---

`Passive Learning` is an inefficient form of learning that involves feeling like you're learning something simply by listening to someone who's already mastered a subject. Failure to put into practice what you want to learn makes the results of this method very poor.

It's more common than we think. In the field of technology, it's an idea closely related to [[Tutorial Hell]].
