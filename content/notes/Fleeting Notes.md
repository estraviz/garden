`Fleeting Notes` are notes intended to capture our thoughts—those we consider valuable or that can help us develop an idea, an action, etc.—as soon as they occur. They can be triggered when our brain triggers an idea, likely because something has evoked a particular thought, or on other occasions, for example, when we read something or see something that evokes an idea we consider interesting or relevant to pursue or explore.
